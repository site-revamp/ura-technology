# URA Technology --- Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

URA Technology connects businesses with experienced product, design, and
technology specialists. Its website is aimed at companies looking to
find an individual specialist, extend an existing team, build a
dedicated team, or develop a digital product.

The redesigned homepage takes a people-led, service-focused approach. It
brings the company's offering, specialist network, and engagement
options into a clearer visual and content hierarchy, while retaining the
approved design direction.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer opening proposition

The homepage gives the visitor a more direct introduction to URA
Technology's talent and digital product services. This addresses the
previous page's reliance on the headline and supporting copy to explain
the breadth of the offering, helping visitors understand the company's
role more quickly.

### 2. More intuitive service discovery

The redesign gives greater prominence to the main ways clients can work
with URA: finding a specialist, extending a team, building a team, or
building a product. This makes the available routes easier to identify
and helps visitors move towards the option that matches their needs.

### 3. Stronger presentation of expertise and credibility

The homepage brings the specialist network, relevant capabilities, and
client references into the overall story. This addresses the opportunity
to connect URA's people and expertise more clearly with the services it
offers, giving prospective clients more context as they evaluate the
company.

## Design Improvements

-   **Visual direction:** Uses the approved supporting palette alongside
    the existing URA brand identity, with a people-led technology
    aesthetic.
-   **Typography:** Applies the approved Space Grotesk heading and DM
    Sans body-font pairing to establish a consistent hierarchy.
-   **Layout and hierarchy:** Organises the homepage around the company
    proposition, service pathways, specialist capabilities, and
    engagement journey.
-   **User experience:** Makes the main client routes and next steps
    easier to scan and understand.
-   **Responsive presentation:** The design direction includes layouts
    intended to adapt across desktop and mobile screens.
