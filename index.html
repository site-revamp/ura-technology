Good to know

A few things you may be wondering.

A little more clarity before you take the next step.

Can I start with just one specialist?

Yes. URA's offer includes connecting companies with an individual expert as well as helping form a wider digital product team.

Can I meet a specialist before making a decision?

URA's published process includes meeting the proposed specialists before deciding to start working together.

What information should I share first?

Start with what you are building, the outcome you want, the skills you need and any important timing considerations. A few details are enough to begin the conversation.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

URA's contact page says the team aims to respond within one working day, then arrange a short call to discuss scope, roles and timing.
